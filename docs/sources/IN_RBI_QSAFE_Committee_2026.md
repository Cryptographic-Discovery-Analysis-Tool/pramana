## Full relevant text

> "Quantum Technology represents a paradigm shift compared with traditional systems as
> it leverages the principles of quantum mechanics, such as superposition and
> entanglement. This enables quantum systems to address complex financial problems such
> as portfolio optimisation, risk assessment, macroeconomic modelling, etc. However,
> quantum technologies can also introduce significant risks, particularly the potential
> to undermine certain existing cryptographic standards.
>
> 2. To examine the related issues, it has been decided to constitute an Expert
> Committee for a Quantum Secure and Adaptive Financial Ecosystem (Q-SAFE)..."

Convener: Dr. Anil Prabhakar, Professor, Department of Electrical Engineering, IIT
Madras. Member-Secretary: Shri Suvendu Pati, CGM, FinTech Department, RBI. Seven other
members (DST, SBI, NPCI, MeitY, DSCI, ex-IBM Quantum India, RBI FinTech).

Terms of reference (para 4), in full:

> "ii. Evaluate the financial sector's cryptographic inventory through a Cryptography
> Bill of Materials (CBOM), assess crypto agility and identify the critical systems and
> processes most vulnerable to such threats.
> ...
> v. Recommend a roadmap and framework to quantum-secure the Indian financial system."

Reporting deadline (para 5):

> "The Committee will submit its report within six months from the date of its first
> meeting."

## Why no dated milestone was added to data/policy_deadlines.yaml

The six-month reporting window is relative to "the date of its first meeting", a date
this press release does not state and that has not been independently located. Per
CLAUDE.md's rule against guessing, no calendar date is derived from it. Recorded in
`data/policy_deadlines.yaml` as `usable: false`, and in `data/sector_profiles.yaml` as
an undated BFSI obligation (financial-sector CBOM inventory under way via Q-SAFE, no
fixed migration deadline yet published).
